01 — About AI/ML

What is Artificial Intelligence
& Machine Learning?

AI is the simulation of human intelligence by machines. ML is a subset where systems learn from data to improve without being explicitly programmed.

🤖

Machine Learning

Algorithms that learn patterns from data to make predictions or decisions — from spam filters to recommendation engines.

🧠

Deep Learning

Neural networks with many layers that can recognize images, understand speech, and generate human-like text.

💬

Natural Language Processing

Teaches machines to read, understand, and generate human language — powering chatbots, translators, and search engines.

Modern AI systems can process millions of data points per second
02 — Data Analysis

The Science of
Making Data Speak

Data Analysis is the process of inspecting, cleaning, and modelling data to discover useful information, draw conclusions, and support decision-making.

1

Collect & Clean

Gather raw data from multiple sources, then remove errors, duplicates, and inconsistencies.

2

Explore & Visualise

Use charts, graphs, and statistics to understand distributions, trends, and outliers in the data.

3

Model & Predict

Apply statistical or ML models to uncover hidden patterns and make reliable predictions.

4

Communicate Insights

Present findings clearly using dashboards and reports that drive real business decisions.
